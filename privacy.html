Privacy Policy

Effective August 15, 2026

PulseOut has no account, ads, tracking, or analytics. Microphone audio is analyzed only on your iPhone and is never recorded or uploaded. Rescue history and preferences stay locally unless you export them. A short-lived Apple DeviceCheck token is used only to prevent repeated free trials and is immediately discarded.

1 About this policy

This policy explains how PulseOut handles information when you use the PulseOut iPhone app, its widgets, support website, and trial-verification service. By using PulseOut, you acknowledge these practices.

2 Information kept on your device

PulseOut stores onboarding status, settings, haptic and background-audio choices, rescue history, elapsed time, volume ranges, ratings, remaining symptoms, statistics, saved tone presets, and the selected default tone on your iPhone. PulseOut does not create an account or send this information to PulseOut servers.

3 Microphone and sound level

Microphone access is requested only after you choose Enable Microphone in Sound Level. Samples are processed in memory to estimate sound pressure level and are discarded immediately. PulseOut does not save, transmit, or share recordings. You may revoke microphone permission at any time in iOS Settings. Sound Level is an estimate and is not a certified sound-level meter.

4 Purchases and the three-day trial

Apple processes the free three-day trial, lifetime purchase, payment, receipt, and restoration through StoreKit under Apple’s policies. PulseOut reads verified transaction identifiers, product identifiers, purchase dates, and entitlement or revocation status only as needed to unlock features. PulseOut does not receive your payment-card details. The free trial does not start a subscription and does not charge automatically when it ends.

5 Trial misuse prevention

When you ask to start or restore a trial, Apple DeviceCheck creates a temporary device token. The app sends that token over an encrypted connection to the PulseOut trial service hosted on Cloudflare, which forwards it to Apple to read or set a single trial-used bit. PulseOut does not use the token for advertising, analytics, tracking, or device fingerprinting. The service does not retain the token, request body, or raw IP address after servicing the request. It may keep a non-reversible token hash for no more than 60 seconds solely to enforce request limits. Apple retains the DeviceCheck bits under its own policies.

6 Exports, support, and external links

History is shared only when you deliberately export a CSV or JSON report through the iOS share sheet. If you contact support, your mail provider and PulseOut receive the information you choose to include. Links to Apple guidance and this online policy open external services governed by their own policies.

7 Service providers and sharing

PulseOut does not sell personal information. Apple provides StoreKit and DeviceCheck, Cloudflare transports trial-verification requests, GitHub Pages hosts this public policy, and your email provider handles messages you initiate. These providers receive only the information necessary to perform those functions and are expected to protect it consistently with their terms and applicable law.

8 Retention and deletion

Local settings and history remain until you clear them, reset the app, or uninstall PulseOut. The app provides a Clear session history action. Trial tokens are discarded after each real-time request and are not placed in a PulseOut database. Apple retains purchase records and DeviceCheck state according to Apple’s policies. Support email may be retained only as long as reasonably necessary to answer and document the request.

9 Security and international processing

PulseOut minimizes transmitted information and uses HTTPS for trial verification. Apple, Cloudflare, GitHub, and email services may process information in countries other than your own under their applicable safeguards. No method of electronic processing is completely risk-free.

10 Children

PulseOut is a general-audience utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because PulseOut has no account, advertising, or analytics, it does not build child profiles. A parent or guardian may contact PulseOut with any concern.

11 Your choices and rights

You can deny or revoke microphone access, clear session history, remove saved tones, choose not to export, dismiss all purchase screens, continue using the free 60-second Rescue, or uninstall the app. Because PulseOut does not maintain user accounts or a personal-data database, it normally has no profile to access or delete. You may contact PulseOut with a privacy inquiry or a request relating to information you supplied through support.

12 Policy changes and contact

PulseOut may update this policy when features, providers, or legal requirements change. Material changes will be reflected by a new effective date in the app and online. Privacy questions may be sent to ronvaisman@gmail.com with the subject “PulseOut Privacy.”
